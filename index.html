ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Omar — I photograph the moments that matter.

I'm a London-based photographer who loves turning real moments into memories you'll keep for good. Whether it's a lively party, an intimate event, or a portrait that captures exactly who someone is, I'm there to catch the honest, in-between moments most people miss.

My style is natural and unposed. I keep things relaxed on the day so you can enjoy the occasion while I do the rest — then hand you a set of images that feel like you.
